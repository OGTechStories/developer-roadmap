# Developer Roadmap

Whether you are a college student starting your journey, a beginner preparing for your first software development job, or an experienced developer looking to change jobs and grow your career, this roadmap is designed to help you.

Start with the fundamentals and gradually move through software development, development tools, databases, APIs, cloud, DevOps, system design, AI, and other skills that can help you become a stronger software developer.

You don't need to learn everything at once. Follow the roadmap step by step, understand the fundamentals, practice what you learn, and go deeper into the areas that match your career goals.

The documentation is currently **a work in progress**. We are adding and improving topics one by one, with practical explanations, examples, projects, and real-world guidance.

**Stay tuned, follow the roadmap, and keep learning as we build it together.**
